# My Passions

I've wanted to write this blog for a long time but I kept holding back :/

Maybe because I'd like to share my life but not my personal information? I guess that's why.

## Code

My passions are varied: I love coding, as you've probably noticed across the rest of this blog. There's something deeply satisfying about building things from scratch, solving problems, and watching your creation come to life. Whether it's a small command-line tool or a full web application, the process never gets old.

I have a particular love for **open source** -- sharing is caring, and I genuinely appreciate the idea that someone else might find my code useful. Most of my projects live on [my GitHub](https://github.com/fox3000foxy), and you can browse them on the [Projects](/projects) page.

## Automating Everything That Moves

I also have a passion for automation. Playing with GitHub Actions and workflows is one of the best feelings I've ever known. Whenever I can replace a manual process with a script or a pipeline, I do it -- no questions asked.

I also recently started signing my commits with an SSH key, and I made a script for that which you can find on [this gist](https://gist.github.com/fox3000foxy/95500d129cd4bf5c173c323d2492569a).

## Discovering Old Technologies

I love discovering old technologies like UTAU -- I find the concept pretty fascinating. There's something special about understanding how people built things before the modern era of AI and cloud computing.

## Vtubers

I also love Vtubers, and I'd like to become one someday, but I'm a bit shy for now. Maybe that'll be for later!

## Music

My main hobby is coding while listening to music. Nothing beats getting into a flow state with the right playlist. I also like going for walks now, not sure why 😕.

## Finding Workarounds

I also love finding workarounds to avoid paying for things -- or at least thinking outside the box. For example, [Nibi](https://github.com/Let-s-Learn-Japanese-Together/Nibi) was one of my latest creations where I found free solutions for command handling, database management, and daemons. It's the challenge that counts, way more than the savings!

## Why This Blog

I wanted this blog to be a place where I can share my progress, my thoughts, and anything I feel like sharing. That way you'll know how I think. And writing about my passions felt like the perfect way to start.

Thanks for reading!
